Zero-Account Privacy Architecture
TapTap requires no user registration, no sign-in, and no profile management. You explore, aggregate, and consume AI news completely anonymously. We do not collect personal identities, we do not sell your data, and your reading preferences remain safely stored on your local device.
Section 01

1. Overview & Zero-Account Architecture

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application TapTap - AI News & Insights (the "Application"), developed and maintained by Firefly Labz ("Company," "we," "our," or "us").

No Account Required: TapTap is engineered as a strictly client-centric, account-free application. We do not support, mandate, or offer user accounts, logins, passwords, social authentication, or centralized profile creation. All readers enjoy identical and unconstrained access without ever sharing identity records or personal contact data.

Under applicable data protection frameworks—including the General Data Protection Regulation (EU/UK GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA (CCPA/CPRA), and Google Play Developer Program Policies—Firefly Labz acts as the Data Controller for any technical telemetry or direct communications initiated by you.

Section 02

2. Information We Collect

Because TapTap does not maintain user accounts, our data collection footprint is minimal and confined to on-device operations and standard diagnostics:

A. User Identification & Account Data (None Collected)

We do not collect names, usernames, email addresses, passwords, phone numbers, or social profile IDs. There is no sign-in or account registration flow in TapTap.

Voluntary Support Correspondence: If you voluntarily reach out to us for technical assistance at fireflylabz@gmail.com, we will receive your sender email address and message contents solely to answer your inquiry.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Diagnostics

Device Specifications: Non-identifying technical parameters (device model, Android OS version, system language, screen resolution) and transient network state.

Stability & Crash Reports: Anonymous crash stacks, non-fatal logs, and ANR traces processed via Firebase Crashlytics to detect software regressions.

Coarse Region: Coarse, country/regional tier IP routing data used strictly to route news feed queries to the closest CDN edge. We do not track or access fine GPS coordinates.

C. On-Device Preferences & Reading Cache

Bookmarks, preferred topic categories (e.g., "Robotics", "LLMs"), and cached articles are stored locally in on-device storage (Android SharedPreferences / SQLite Cache). This information is not synchronized to a remote account profile.

Absolute Non-Collection Guarantee

TapTap does not track, inspect, or request access to: Contacts, Biometrics, Precise GPS Location, Financial Details, Gallery/Photos, or Background Microphones.

Section 03

3. AI Processing & Usage Disclosures

Because TapTap operates as an autonomous artificial intelligence news aggregator, we are committed to complete transparency regarding algorithmic processing:

  • Content Summarization: Our proprietary Large Language Model ingestion pipeline summarizes public research papers (ArXiv, BioRxiv), technical press releases, and code repository commits. User queries submitted to the "Ask TapTap" agent are transmitted in an ephemeral state strictly to answer your question and are purged after generation.
  • No Public Foundation Model Training: We explicitly warrant that your private personal readings, search queries, and bookmarks are never used to train public foundational AI models without explicit prior opt-in consent.
  • Zero Data Brokerage: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers, marketers, or external advertisers.
  • Local First Personalization: Recommendation vectors for topic tailoring are processed client-side or tied to pseudonymous user IDs where possible.
