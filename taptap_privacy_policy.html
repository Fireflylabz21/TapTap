Summary for TapTap Users
TapTap is built on privacy-by-design. We do not sell your personal data, we do not track precise GPS locations, and we do not use your reading activity to train public AI models without consent. You maintain full ownership of your data and can request instantaneous deletion at any time.
Section 01

1. Overview & Data Controller

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application TapTap - AI News & Insights (the "Application"), developed and maintained by TapTap Technologies Inc. ("Company," "we," "our," or "us").

Under global data protection regulations—including the General Data Protection Regulation (EU/UK GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), and Google Play Developer Program Policies—TapTap Technologies Inc. acts as the Data Controller responsible for the processing of your personal data.

By installing, accessing, or using TapTap, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and consented to the data practices described herein. If you do not agree with this policy, please uninstall the Application and refrain from using our services.

Section 02

2. Information We Collect

To provide automated AI curation, deliver audio summaries, and maintain reliable application performance, we collect specific categories of information:

A. Information You Provide Voluntarily

Account Credentials: If you choose to create an account, authenticate via Google Sign-In, or join the VIP Beta, we collect your verified email address, display name, and authentication tokens. Registration is optional; guest browsing is supported.

Customer Support & Direct Inquiries: Any feedback, bug reports, or correspondence sent through our in-app feedback modal or via email.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Data

Device & Hardware Telemetry: Device brand and model, operating system version (e.g., Android 15), system language, display resolution, and anonymous application instance IDs.

Network Diagnostics & IP: Coarse geolocation (country and city level derived from IP address for localized tech news feeds). We never collect granular GPS or fine location.

Crash & Performance Telemetry: Stack traces, ANR (Application Not Responding) metrics, and memory diagnostics collected via Firebase Crashlytics to resolve bugs.

C. App Usage & Preferences

Reading bookmarks, custom topic tags (e.g., "LLMs", "Silicon"), audio briefing playback duration, and read/dismiss interactions. This data is utilized strictly to train your personal on-device recommendation weights.

Sensitive Data Non-Collection Guarantee

TapTap does not collect or process: Biometric data (facial scans, fingerprints), financial card numbers, health or medical data, precise GPS coordinates, contact address books, microphones (outside of explicit active voice search permissions), or camera feeds.

Section 03

3. AI Processing & Usage Disclosures

Because TapTap operates as an autonomous artificial intelligence news aggregator, we are committed to complete transparency regarding algorithmic processing:

  • Content Summarization: Our proprietary Large Language Model ingestion pipeline summarizes public research papers (ArXiv, BioRxiv), technical press releases, and code repository commits. User queries submitted to the "Ask TapTap" agent are transmitted in an ephemeral state strictly to answer your question and are purged after generation.
  • No Public Foundation Model Training: We explicitly warrant that your private personal readings, search queries, and bookmarks are never used to train public foundational AI models without explicit prior opt-in consent.
  • Zero Data Brokerage: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers, marketers, or external advertisers.
  • Local First Personalization: Recommendation vectors for topic tailoring are processed client-side or tied to pseudonymous user IDs where possible.

Section 05

5. Data Retention, Storage & Security

We enforce rigorous cryptographic protocols designed to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure:

Encryption In Transit

All data transferred between your mobile client and our servers is strictly encapsulated via TLS 1.3 cryptographic suites.

Encryption At Rest

User database clusters and backups are encrypted utilizing industry-standard AES-256 with rotating key management.

Retention Periods: Telemetry and crash logs are retained for a rolling window of 90 days before automated deletion. Account information and saved bookmarks are preserved until you trigger an account deletion request.

Section 06

6. Your Legal Rights (GDPR, UK & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your place of residence, you possess enforceable statutory rights:

• Right of Access & Portability: Obtain a copy of your stored personal information in a machine-readable JSON format.
• Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete profile records.
• Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request unconditional deletion of all personal identifiers, bookmarks, and logs.
• Right to Opt-Out: Exercise "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Info" (TapTap naturally complies by default).

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

TapTap is directed at adults, AI researchers, software engineers, and technology professionals. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 within the European Economic Area).

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe your child has submitted personal details to TapTap, please contact our Data Protection Officer immediately at privacy@taptap-ai.com, and we will take expeditious steps to delete such records from our infrastructure.
